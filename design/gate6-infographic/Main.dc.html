One trusted, AI-enabled workspace per engagement, on Karta’s delivery methodology
What it is
A page each project team opens to see where the engagement stands, what needs attention, and what to do next.
Home follows the methodology. Seven dated stages, the next control point, stage deliverables, and the actions recommended for right now.
Weekly Status in four steps. Review sources, draft, approve under your name, generate the Karta deck. Status is derived in code, never guessed.
Ask Claude about the project. Answers come from the Asana project and the approved record, cite what they used, and say when evidence is missing.
A record that lasts. Decisions, approved values, and generated decks stay with the engagement, versioned and attributed.

1 · REVIEW
Review sources
Milestones with derived status, source freshness, and gaps recorded with the deck.
2 · DRAFT
Claude drafts, on a click
Narrative and suggested assessments from the evidence. Code does dates and rollups.
3 · APPROVE
A named person approves
Corrects the draft, then takes responsibility. Never skippable. Corrections are measured.
4 · GENERATE
The Karta deck is built
From approved values only, on the KCG template, into the engagement’s status folder.
One workspace per engagement
Independent by design. Nothing crosses between clients.
Karta-hosted assembly
Approved values in, deck out. Holds no credentials, keeps no client data.
Nothing is sent
Distribution is a person’s choice, outside the workspace.
Rules that do not bend
Reads as you. Every connector call uses the viewer’s own authorization. No service account, no shared credential.
The approval gate is never skipped. No client-facing value leaves without a named human correcting the draft first.
Missing evidence is never Green. It shows as Unknown or Not assessed and blocks a Green rollup.
Code derives, the model drafts. Dates, variance, status and the file are computed. The model writes prose and recommends.
Sources stay the source of truth. The workspace reads Asana, SharePoint, Granola and Harvest. It never competes with them.
Measured in the pilot: time from draft to approval, drafted fields the reviewer corrected, adoption, and errors found after approval.

Stage 1 Prove
Built September 4 to 8, 2026. Live today: the blank workspace template, the hosted assembly service, and the first real engagement set up in about ten minutes.
Standing up an engagement
1
Publish a blank workspace and hand it to the initial owner, usually the CSE.
2
The owner completes setup: names, the Asana project link, the SharePoint folder link, Granola and Harvest from lists. Each is checked live.
3
The team connects their own sources and opens the page. Under an hour each.
What happens next
Stage 1 Prove. Weekly Status every week on two engagements for three to four weeks.
Tune on evidence. The drafting prompt improves against measured corrections, not taste.
Then expand. Sprint review, meeting capture, UAT preparation and onboarding briefs follow the same approval pattern.
