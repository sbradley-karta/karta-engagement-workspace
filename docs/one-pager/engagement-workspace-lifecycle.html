Karta AI Engagement Workspace

Karta's methodology, with a digital partner at every stage.

The Engagement Workspace puts Claude inside each stage of how Karta delivers. It reads the engagement's own evidence, drafts Karta's artifacts in Karta's method, and remembers every decision for the next activity. Code calculates, people approve, and nothing is sent by software. Below: the two highest-value workflows at each stage, and where each stands.

Visible time saved in week one; the plan becomes the milestones tracked from then on.
