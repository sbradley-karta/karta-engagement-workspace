Karta AI Engagement Workspace

Open Monday already knowing where your engagement stands.

Every Karta engagement gets its own AI-enabled workspace: the plan, the evidence, the decisions and the next step in one place, shaped by Karta's methodology, with a named person accountable for every output. Claude reads the engagement's evidence, drafts Karta's artifacts and remembers every decision. Code calculates, people approve, nothing is sent by software.

  • See where you standThe Karta lifecycle with real dates, the next control point, and whether its evidence is ready.
  • Know what needs attentionSlipped milestones, open decisions, capacity gaps. Calculated from the sources, not typed in.
  • Do the week's work without rebuilding the contextStatus, sprint reviews, UAT scripts and briefs prepared from the same approved record.

Real, and running. First workspace live for Legend on September 8, 2026; first Weekly Status approved and the deck built the next day. Set-up takes about ten minutes.
