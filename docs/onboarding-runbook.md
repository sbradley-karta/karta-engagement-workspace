# Member onboarding runbook

**Time:** under one hour. **Who:** any Karta member staffed on the engagement. **Owner:** the engagement owner walks the first member through it; after that the page's Sources chips tell a member what is missing.

## Before you start

You need a seat on the Karta Claude Team account and to be staffed on the engagement in Asana and the engagement folder in SharePoint. The workspace reads as you; if you cannot see the project in Asana, the workspace cannot either.

## Steps

1. **Connect your sources, once.** In claude.ai, open Customize, then Connectors. Connect Asana, Microsoft 365, Granola, and Harvest with your own Karta accounts. Then find Karta Assembly and click Connect. During the interim it needs no sign-in; after the tenant admin approves the Karta Assembly Service, it will ask you to sign in with your Karta Microsoft account once and accept the file permission.
2. **Open the engagement page** from the link the engagement owner shared. The first time, the viewer asks you to allow the page to use your connectors and to ask Claude on your seat. Allow both.
3. **Read the rail.** Left side: Home, Weekly status, Milestones, RAID, Decisions, Artifacts, Catalog, Config. At the bottom, six source chips. Green means reading as you; grey dashed means not connected; red dashed means missing evidence. Click a chip for the fix.
4. **Read Home.** Where the project is, the next control point, the stage deliverables, what needs attention, and what was approved. Nothing here is generated by the model; it is read from sources and the approved record.
5. **Run Weekly status when it is your turn.** Review sources, Draft, correct, Approve under your own name and email, Generate deck. During the interim the deck downloads to your computer and you upload it to the Status and Steer Co folder yourself; later it saves to SharePoint directly.
6. **Ask Claude** from the top bar for questions about the project. Answers cite what they used and say when evidence is missing. Nothing you ask is retained unless you save it.

## If something is off

- A chip is red or grey: click it and follow the fix. Most fixes are a connector you have not connected yet.
- Home says you are outside the Claude viewer: open the link in claude.ai, not in a plain browser tab.
- Generate deck fails: the message names the cause and an owner. Message the support route shown in Config.

## What you are accountable for

Anything you approve under your name may reach a client. Check every value. Missing evidence shows as Unknown or Not assessed and must never be turned Green without a reason you can state.
